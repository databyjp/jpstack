Audit ${@:-the most consequential claim or recommendation from the current conversation}.

Check:

- Whether current first-party documentation supports the stated capability
- Whether the supporting documentation or product behavior is outdated
- Whether independent users or developers corroborate it
- Whether the project has meaningful adoption and active maintenance
- Whether cited third-party sources are reputable and relevant
- Whether contrary evidence or stronger alternatives were omitted
- Whether the recommendation overstates the available evidence

Quote or cite the exact passages that determine the result. Separate facts from interpretation and state any unresolved uncertainty.

Conclude with one verdict:

- Supported
- Supported with qualifications
- Weakly supported
- Unsupported
- Outdated

Explain the verdict concisely and correct the original claim when necessary.
